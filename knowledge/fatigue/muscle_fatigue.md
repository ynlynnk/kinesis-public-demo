# Understanding muscle fatigue

## Plain-language explanation

Muscle fatigue is a task-related reduction in a muscle's ability to produce force or continue performing at the same level.

It is not necessarily the same as the person feeling generally tired. A person may report tiredness without a measurable muscle-fatigue pattern, or measurable changes may occur before the person reports feeling tired.

## How KINESIS should explain its result

A KINESIS fatigue result is an AI classification based on the sensor input provided to Model 1.

- "No fatigue detected" means the model did not identify its learned fatigue pattern.
- "Fatigue detected" means the model identified a pattern associated with fatigue.
- Fatigue probability represents the model's confidence in its classification.
- Fatigue probability does not measure the severity of a patient's condition.
- Estimated fatigue onset indicates when the model first identified the pattern.
- Estimated onset is not necessarily the exact physiological beginning of fatigue.

## Surface EMG and fatigue

Surface electromyography, or sEMG, measures electrical activity associated with muscle activation.

During a fatiguing task, changes can occur in characteristics such as signal amplitude and frequency. However, these changes can vary depending on:

- the muscle being measured;
- the exercise or movement;
- contraction type and intensity;
- electrode placement;
- signal quality;
- the individual being assessed; and
- the measurement protocol.

One measurement should not be interpreted as a clinical diagnosis by itself.

## Comparing rehabilitation sessions

Comparisons are most meaningful when sessions use similar:

- exercises;
- sensor placement;
- recording procedures;
- movement duration; and
- task intensity.

A change between sessions should be described as an observed difference. It should not automatically be called an improvement or deterioration.

## Safe explanation examples

Use:

- "No fatigue pattern was detected during this session."
- "The AI detected a pattern associated with muscle fatigue."
- "The result should be interpreted together with the session conditions and the physiotherapist's assessment."
- "The fatigue probability describes the model's confidence, not the severity of fatigue."

Avoid:

- "The patient definitely has muscle fatigue."
- "The patient has recovered."
- "The rehabilitation treatment should be changed."
- "The percentage shows how severe the fatigue is."

## Important limitation

This information provides general background only. It must not override patient-specific evidence or the physiotherapist's professional judgement.

KINESIS results support professional review and are not a medical diagnosis.
